Generated images are concept visuals, not documentary photographs of a real FORMA studio. Third-party photographs are credited by their sources.

- `assets/bmw-m5-g90.jpg` — generated concept artwork of a black BMW M5 G90; not documentary photography and not evidence of work performed by FORMA.
- `assets/paint-correction.jpg` — Zac Nielson, “Man in white t-shirt standing beside red car,” Unsplash. https://unsplash.com/photos/man-in-white-t-shirt-standing-beside-red-car-q94A6k81lAQ (Unsplash License).
- `assets/window-tint-forma.jpg` — generated concept image of a gloved installer applying tint film to a side window.
- `assets/window-tint-real.jpg` — legacy Pexels photo, no longer used on the page.
- `assets/polish-comparison-wide.jpg` and `assets/polish-comparison-tight.jpg` — matched generated before/after concept image crops for desktop and mobile.
- `assets/polish-comparison.webp` — legacy image from Le Perfectionniste, Metz (Nietzsche92 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0). No longer used on the page.

The Pexels and Unsplash licenses permit use in this project. Their photographers and depicted studios are not affiliated with or endorsing FORMA.
